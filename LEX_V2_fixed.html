03 / THE PATH

From curiosity
to courtroom.

A future-facing path, not a claim that the destination is already reached. The point is to show the direction: learn the law, understand the system, then build inside it.
NOW / 10

Build the foundation.

School, debate, public speaking, technology and learning how to defend an idea without confusing confidence with correctness.

NEXT / 11–12

Choose the questions.

Commerce, economics, technology and the subjects that sharpen the ability to understand how systems actually work.

LATER / LLB

Study the machinery.

Law becomes more than a career label: constitutional law, technology, rights, policy, contracts and the reasoning behind them.

FUTURE / ∞

Build at the intersection.

Legal thinking + technology + entrepreneurship. The long game is not simply knowing the rules. It is understanding why they exist and where they need to evolve.
